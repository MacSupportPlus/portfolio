bet-edge-app.netlify.app/this-week/nfl
Personal project, sports analytics

A probability model that sizes every pick by its edge

Each week it pulls the upcoming NFL and college football games with live odds, runs them through my own model, and shows only the games where the model's probability beats the market's, with a stake sized to the bankroll.

  • Probability model: Elo ratings plus team-stat features like yards, turnover margin, and offensive efficiency, compared against the market's implied probability with the bookmaker's margin included.
  • Kelly-criterion sizing turns each edge into a recommended stake, and a tracker logs results and closing-line value over time.
  • Guardrails against overconfidence: when the model disagrees with the market by too much, the pick is flagged as a model limitation instead of being recommended.
